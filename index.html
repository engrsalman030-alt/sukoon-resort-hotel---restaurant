A lush green paradise surrounded by snow-capped peaks. Perfect for day trips.

A crystal-clear alpine lake tucked deep in the mountains. A must-see for nature lovers.

From Blue Water: 7-8h Trek Difficulty: Hard

The ultimate hidden gem. Remote, quiet, and absolutely breathtaking.
